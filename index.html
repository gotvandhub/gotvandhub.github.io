About Me

I am a researcher working at the intersection of Wireless Communications, UAV Communications, Machine Learning, and Game Theory. My research interests focus on applying intelligent learning and game-theoretic approaches to address challenging problems in next-generation wireless communication networks.

I am particularly interested in leveraging machine learning, reinforcement learning, large language models and game theory to develop intelligent, adaptive, and efficient communication frameworks for UAV-assisted networks. My broader research vision is to explore security challenges in UAV communications, investigate emerging vehicular networks, and study the integration of 6G technologies with UAV-assisted communication systems.

I aim to contribute to the development of secure, reliable, and intelligent wireless networks by exploring the interplay between communication technologies, autonomous systems, and intelligent decision-making. I am also interested in emerging research directions that connect UAV communications, vehicular networks, and 6G-enabled intelligent networking.
