## Profile Highlights

- **Research Expertise and Leadership:** Eight years of intensive research experience in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and UAV-assisted communication systems, with a strong record of research innovation and scientific contributions. Demonstrated research leadership through initiating, developing, and coordinating multidisciplinary research activities, frequently serving as first author and leading collaborative publications in prestigious IEEE journals and international conferences.
- **International Collaboration and Research Supervision:** Led and co-led international research initiatives involving researchers and collaborators from Portugal, Canada, Iran, Australia, USA and China. Experienced in establishing research collaborations, defining research directions, providing intellectual and methodological guidance, coordinating multidisciplinary teams, and mentoring research collaborators.
- **Publications and Research Impact:** Authored and co-authored 25 scientific publications in peer-reviewed international journals and conferences, with a strong citation record and a high h-index, demonstrating sustained research productivity and international scientific impact.
- **Research Funding and Project Development:** Actively contributed to an externally funded research project supported by prestigious funding bodies, including the Portuguese Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) and the European Union.
- **Teaching and Academic Experience:** Experienced in undergraduate teaching, including serving as a Unit Leader for BSc courses at Azad University of Shooshtar and Payame Noor University.
- **Awards and Recognition:** Recipient of several academic and professional distinctions, including:
  - Silver Medal, British Invention Society.
  - Best Student Award in Mathematics and Physics.
- **Industry Experience:** Two years of professional experience contributing to enterprise-level projects, providing practical exposure to industrial problem-solving, technology development, and the application of engineering expertise in real-world environments.
- **Interview:** Featured in a Radio Javan interview in 2007 discussing one of my patented inventions.

## Research Interests

- Research interests lie at the intersection of wireless communications, UAV communications, machine learning, and game theory, with a focus on applying reinforcement learning, large language models (LLMs), and LLM-centric agentic AI to develop intelligent, autonomous, and adaptive solutions for UAV networks, emphasizing communication optimization and resource allocation.
- Future research plans focus on advancing the security and resilience of UAV-assisted sensor networks, exploring innovative applications of UAVs in beyond-5G and 6G wireless networks, and investigating the integration of UAV technologies into vehicular networks.
