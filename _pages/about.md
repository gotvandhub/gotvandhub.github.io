I am a researcher at the intersection of **wireless communications, UAV communications, machine learning, and game theory**. My research focuses on applying intelligent learning and game-theoretic approaches to address challenging problems in next-generation wireless communication networks.

I am interested in leveraging machine learning, reinforcement learning, and game theory to develop intelligent, adaptive, and efficient communication frameworks for UAV-assisted networks. My broader research vision is to explore security challenges in UAV communications, investigate emerging vehicular networks, and study the integration of 6G technologies with UAV-assisted communication systems.

My research interests also extend to emerging AI paradigms, including large language models and agentic AI, particularly their potential applications in intelligent UAV systems. I aim to contribute to secure, reliable, and intelligent wireless networks through research connecting communication technologies, autonomous systems, and intelligent decision-making.

## Research interests
- Wireless communications
- UAV communications
- LLM
- DRL
- Game Theory
- 6G
- Vehicular Networks
